## Procurement Pillars

1. **Zero upfront CapEx** — concessionaires fund 100% of infrastructure, hardware (weighbridges, POS, GNSS), edge sensors, and customization; capital amortized through a declining share of incremental verified IGR.
2. **Sovereign data & open core** — permissive licensing (MIT/Apache-2.0); all citizen, land, fiscal, and asset data resident in-country per NDPA 2023.
3. **Programmatic statutory splits** — every transaction split automatically at settlement into CRF, MDA retention, and concessionaire escrow; no vendor touches un-split gross funds.
4. **Multi-state interoperability** — standardized core microservices across states: repeatable codebases for vendors, shared maintenance and cross-border e-waybill verification for states.
