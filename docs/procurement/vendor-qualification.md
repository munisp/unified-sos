# Vendor Qualification Matrix & Mandatory Pre-Requisites

## 3.1 Mandatory Eligibility Criteria (Pass/Fail Hurdle)

Bidders must satisfy **100%** of the following at pre-qualification; failure on any single item is immediate disqualification.

| Area | Mandatory Criterion & Evidence | Statutory Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Legal Registration | Certificate of Incorporation (CAC Nigeria); Mem & Arts showing IT/Engineering objects | CAMA 2020 |
| Tax Compliance | Valid FIRS Tax Clearance Certificate for the immediate past 3 consecutive years | Public Procurement Act 2007, s.16(6) |
| Pension & Social Security | Valid PENCOM Compliance Certificate and ITF Clearance Certificate | Pension Reform Act 2014; ITF Act |
| Data Protection | NDPC License as verified Data Protection Compliance Organization (DPCO) or valid annual NDPA audit filing | NDPA 2023, s.5 |
| Information Security | Active ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 22301 accreditations | NITDA Information Security Framework |
| Nigerian Local Content | ≥60% Nigerian indigenous engineering workforce; in-country Tier-3/Tier-4 hosting agreements | NITDA Nigerian Content Guidelines |
| Financial Solvency & Escrow | 3-year audited accounts (positive net worth) + Tier-1 bank guarantee: ₦500m (Lots 3, 4, 6) / ₦200m (Lots 1, 2, 5, 7, 8) | ICRC Concession Bankability Benchmark |
