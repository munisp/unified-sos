# Legal, Statutory & Regulatory Compliance Baseline

The SOS procurement and delivery framework strictly satisfies Nigerian public finance and digital sovereignty standards.

## Statutory Instruments

| Instrument | SOS Alignment |
|---|---|
| **ICRC Act 2005** | Concession structuring, risk allocation matrices, value-for-money benchmarks, competitive bidding; QCBS (70% technical / 30% commercial) concessionaire selection |
| **State PPP & Public Procurement Laws** | NASIDA (Nasarawa) · TARIPA (Taraba) · Ogun PPP Law (Dec 2019) + standing PPP Office · Osun user-fee concession law · BIPC/BDIC + state procurement law (Benue) · Lagos concession practice (Lekki Port, Adiyan II, Imota) |
| **NDPA 2023** | Mandatory in-country data residency within certified Tier-3 Nigerian data centers; strict prohibition of extraterritorial citizen PII transfer; vendor acts solely as Data Processor |
| **NITDA Guidelines** | 100% open-source licensing core with quarterly source-code escrow and civil-service engineering shadow teams; ≥60% Nigerian indigenous engineering workforce |
| **CAMA 2020 / PPA 2007** | Vendor legal registration and tax compliance eligibility |

## Module-Level Statutory Anchoring

Every deployed module is anchored on specific statutory instruments for non-repudiation and election-cycle durability:

- **ICRC-compliant PPP framework:** concessions under state PPP laws, ring-fencing vendor investments through statutory revenue shares (8–18%) deducted at source by TigerBeetle.
- **Consolidated Revenue / TSA edicts:** gazetted orders that all collections flow into the State TSA, eliminating manual departmental cash accounts.
- **Mining & environmental inter-governmental MoUs:** joint frameworks between the Federal Ministry of Solid Minerals / NEITI and State Mineral Agencies permitting state digital custody checkpoints.
- **NDPA compliance:** biometric and land cadastre databases in sovereign Nigerian cloud environments with role-based cryptographic masking.

## Election-Cycle Institutionalization

- Anchor concessions in **statutes and published frameworks** (TAGIS statute, NASIDA law, Ogun 2019 PPP Law, Osun PPP law) rather than executive goodwill — the Nasarawa "Lafia Declaration" precedent is the hardening model.
- Every concession carries **successor-administration continuity clauses** and escrowed, auditable revenue-sharing mechanics any incoming government can verify rather than re-litigate.
- ICRC-compliant documentation from day one, keeping federal pathways open without taking ICRC timelines onto the critical path.
